Weather smarts

Your minutes per cycle and soak never change. A hot, dry day runs more cycles, a cool or damp one fewer, and wet soil skips the run.

ET is the water the garden loses in a day to heat, dryness, wind and sun. "Normal ET" is the day that gets your usual cycles. Pots and beds are about 1.0, lawn about 0.8.
